📖 Craft Philosophy02 // PHILOSOPHY
🍎 DESIGN SKILL:

I enjoy creating designs that adapt to different concepts and visual identities. My skills include logo design, branding, illustration, layout, and visual composition. I like experimenting with typography, shapes, colors, and imagery to create designs that are both visually appealing and suited to their intended purpose.

🎨 ART STYLE:

My personal art style focuses on expressive, painterly illustrations with textured brushwork, bold colors, and stylized characters. I enjoy using color and lighting to create atmosphere and emotion rather than focusing purely on realistic rendering. While I like experimenting with different techniques and visual approaches, my work often maintains a soft, organic quality with expressive features and strong color choices. My style continues to develop as I explore new ways of combining color, texture, and character design.
